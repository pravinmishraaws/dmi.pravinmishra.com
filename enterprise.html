The Problem

Most DevOps training doesn't survive contact with your codebase

Teams finish the course, the certificates go in a folder, and nothing ships differently the following month. Three reasons it keeps happening.

Watched, not done

Video completion gets reported as progress. Nobody wrote a pipeline, broke a cluster, or fixed it — so none of it holds under pressure.

Generic by default

The same deck for every client, on tooling your team may not run. Engineers spend the workshop mentally translating it to their own stack.

No evidence afterwards

An attendance list is not a capability report. You can't see who actually got it, who is stuck, or what changed — so you can't justify doing it again.

How It Runs

Five steps, starting with your outcome

Every engagement starts from what your team needs to ship, not from a fixed syllabus.

1

Discover

A call to establish the business outcome, the stack you actually run, and who needs to be trained.

2

Diagnose

Benchmark the team against the DMI assignments to find the real gaps, rather than assuming them.

3

Design

Select and sequence the weeks that close those gaps, on your tooling and your cloud.

4

Deliver

Self-paced, live cohort, or the advanced instructor-led track — with assignments graded automatically.

5

Measure

Per-engineer progress reporting your managers can read, plus the GitHub work itself as evidence.
