Accreditation & Compliance

Built for your NAAC / NBA reporting — and your NEP 2020 mandate

All three are shifting toward exactly the kind of evidence a DMI partnership produces.

🎓

NEP 2020 — Skill-Credit Mandate

India's National Education Policy requires vocational/skill credits as part of every degree program — not optional, not just a scoring boost. Most colleges don't yet have a real way to deliver that. DMI is a ready-made, verifiable skill-credit track your students can start on immediately.

📊

NAAC — Student Support & Progression

Criterion V is a 100-point weighted component of the institutional SSR, scored on placement outcomes, progression, and employability-skill development. A campus partnership generates real, per-student completion and placement data you can cite directly.

✅

NBA — Verified Graduate Competencies

NBA's revised SAR format (aligned to GAPC v4.0) explicitly calls for demonstrable, verified graduate competencies — not just completed coursework. DMI's daily-graded, auditable output is built for exactly that standard.

Exact reporting formats vary by institution and accreditation cycle — tell us what your current SSR/SAR/NEP-compliance needs are, and we'll align our campus report to it.
